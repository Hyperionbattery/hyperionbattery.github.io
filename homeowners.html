For homeowners

Your golf cart could back up your home


A golf cart carries a sizable battery that sits parked most of the day. We're developing a way to put that battery to work keeping your essential circuits running during an outage, so the cart you already own does double duty.

Join the early access list

Where this started: one battery for your home and your equipment


Hyperion began with a simple question: what is your golf cart, mower, or e-bike battery doing when you aren't using it? Nothing. We built a prototype with swappable packs that move between a wall-mounted rack, a golf cart, and an e-bike, and recharge from solar.


That work now feeds two directions: managed and bidirectional charging for golf course fleets, and home backup for golf cart owners.

Prototype Gallery


We built a prototype from off-the-shelf bike batteries and other hardware to show batteries swapping from powering a shed, to an e-bike, to a golf cart, and recharging with solar panels. The prototype uses 4 battery packs which slide in and out of the rack horizontally.

Do not try this at home. It looks simple, but it needs a custom energy management system to keep the batteries from discharging into each other and overheating.
