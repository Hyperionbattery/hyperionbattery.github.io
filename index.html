Why swappable


Batteries sitting idle in equipment are wasted capital. Hyperion packs are swappable between all supported equipment and charge in a rack mounted in your garage, cart barn, or maintenance building, so the same batteries do double duty: they move your vehicles and they store energy for your building.


Swapping takes seconds, which means no charging downtime, more efficient use of DC solar power, and fewer batteries to buy overall. It will be the most flexible and modular battery energy storage system available.
