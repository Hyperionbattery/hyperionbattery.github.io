Managed charging for golf course fleets

Electrify your cart fleet without waiting on a service upgrade


Hyperion schedules your whole fleet's charging to fit inside the electrical capacity you already have. Adding carts, switching to lithium, or putting in new chargers doesn't have to mean a new panel, a new transformer, and months of waiting on the utility.

When every cart plugs in at once, your electrical service feels it


  • At the end of the day, the whole fleet goes back to the barn and plugs in at the same time.
  • Every charger pulling at once creates one big peak, and your panel and service have to be sized for that peak.
  • Growing the fleet, converting gas carts, or upgrading to lithium chargers can push you past what your service can carry.
  • The usual fix is an electrical upgrade: new equipment, permits, and a long wait for the utility.

But carts sit parked for hours every night. They don't all need to charge at the same moment. They just need to be full by the first tee time.

How it works


1 Measure

We meter your cart barn to see how much electrical capacity you're really using, and when.

2 Manage (V1G)

Hyperion paces and staggers charging across the fleet so the total load stays under a limit you set, and every cart is still full by morning.

3 Give back (V2G) In development

Your carts are big batteries that sit parked most of the night. Bidirectional charging will let them support your clubhouse or barn when power is expensive or the grid goes down.
