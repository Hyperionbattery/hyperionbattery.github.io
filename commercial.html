Your cart fleet is a battery. Put it to work on your power bill.


Hyperion swappable battery packs charge your golf carts when electricity is cheap and send power back to the clubhouse, pump house, and maintenance shop when electricity is expensive. Same carts, same rounds, lower bill.


Get a Savings Estimate

How it works


  1. Charge off-peak. Overnight and midday, when your utility's rates are at their lowest, the swapping racks charge every pack in the fleet.
  2. Play all day. Carts run on charged packs. When a pack runs low, staff swap in a fresh one in seconds instead of parking the cart on a charger.
  3. Discharge on-peak. During the expensive late-afternoon peak, the racks stop drawing power and feed the packs' stored energy back into your course's electrical load.
  4. Repeat daily. The system runs on your utility's rate schedule automatically, every day of the season.

Where the savings come from


Energy arbitrage

Every kilowatt-hour your course uses during peak hours can instead be bought overnight at the off-peak rate. You pay the cheap price for power you use at the expensive time.

Peak shaving and demand charges

Commercial bills are driven by your highest 15-minute demand spike, not just total usage. Discharging the fleet during those spikes — and never charging carts into them — pulls your peak down and takes the demand charge with it.

More value from your solar

Already have panels, or considering them? Store the midday surplus in the cart packs instead of exporting it for a low export credit, then use it after the sun goes down.

Backup power for the course

When the grid goes down, the same packs keep the clubhouse, POS, irrigation, and refrigeration running so play and food service continue.

No new real estate

The storage is the fleet you already buy. No separate container of batteries, no ground lease, no dedicated pad next to the maintenance building.

No cart downtime

Swapping takes seconds, so a pack that spent the afternoon powering the clubhouse never keeps a cart off the first tee.

Built around how a course actually runs


  • Packs swap between carts, utility vehicles, and the charging rack — one battery standard across the property
  • Racks mount in the cart barn, so charging and discharging happen where the fleet already parks
  • The energy management system decides when to charge and discharge from your utility's rate schedule
  • Fleet packs stay reserved for play first; only surplus capacity is sold back into the course load
  • Scales a pack at a time as you replace or expand the fleet, instead of one large capital purchase
