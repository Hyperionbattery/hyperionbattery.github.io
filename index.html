Why batteries should do double duty


Batteries sitting idle in equipment are wasted capital. A lawnmower battery does nothing between mows; a golf cart battery does nothing overnight. Meanwhile the building next to them buys expensive power off the grid.


Hyperion closes that gap two ways. For homes, we are building swappable packs that move between your equipment and a charging rack that backs up the house. For golf courses, we are building bidirectional chargers that let the batteries already in your carts power the course when electricity is at its most expensive.
