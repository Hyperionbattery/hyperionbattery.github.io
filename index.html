When every cart plugs in at once, your electrical service feels it


  • At the end of the day, the whole fleet goes back to the barn and plugs in at the same time.
  • Every charger pulling at once creates one big peak, and your panel and service have to be sized for that peak.
  • Growing the fleet, converting gas carts, or upgrading to lithium chargers can push you past what your service can carry.
  • The usual fix is an electrical upgrade: new equipment, permits, and a long wait for the utility.

And if cart charging sets your monthly demand peak, you pay for it on every electric bill too.


But carts sit parked for hours every night. They don't all need to charge at the same moment. They just need to be full by the first tee time.

How it works


1 Measure

We meter your cart barn to see how much electrical capacity you're really using, and when.

2 Manage (V1G)

Hyperion paces and staggers charging across the fleet so the total load stays under a limit you set, and every cart is still full by morning.

3 Give back (V2G) In development

Your carts are big batteries that sit parked most of the night. Bidirectional charging will let them support your clubhouse or barn when power is expensive or the grid goes down.

Common questions


Does the assessment change anything on my carts or chargers?

No. The bill review is just paperwork, and the meter only measures how much power your cart barn uses and when. Your carts and chargers keep working exactly as they do today.

What does it cost?

The bill review, the metering, and the report are free. If managed charging looks like a fit for your course, we'll talk through pilot terms with you before anything else happens.

What do you need from us?

A short call, 12 months of electric bills, and access to your cart barn panel for the meter install. We schedule the install around your operations.

Is managed charging allowed by electrical code?

The National Electrical Code includes provisions for energy management systems that cap total load. Approval is up to your local inspector, and we work through that with you before anything is installed.

Will it work with our carts?

The metering works with any fleet. Whether managed charging works with your chargers is one of the things the assessment checks.

Who is Hyperion?

Hyperion is an early-stage company founded by Matt Ashcroft, who holds a PhD in sustainable engineering focused on energy systems. We're working closely with a small number of pilot courses to build managed and bidirectional charging for cart fleets.
